I need

Everything you need to know before you go

Every listing answers the questions you would otherwise have to call ahead and ask.

  • Mobility access

    Step-free entrances, ramp details, door widths, table heights and space to move between tables.

  • Readable menus

    Braille, large-print and screen-reader friendly digital menus, with allergen and ingredient info.

  • Sensory friendly

    Noise levels by time of day, lighting notes and quiet hours for diners with sensory needs.

  • Verified by diners

    Photos and reviews from people with disabilities, so you know the details are real and up to date.

  • Book with your needs

    Tell the restaurant what you need when you reserve, so your table is ready when you arrive.

  • Accessible directions

    Routes from transit and parking to the front door, avoiding stairs and steep slopes.
